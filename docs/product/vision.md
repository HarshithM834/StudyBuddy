# Product vision

Version 0.2 · October 16, 2026 · [Product baseline](README.md)

**StudyBuddy helps students understand and apply what their course teaches by keeping a personal tutor, their course material, and their learning progress together where they study.**

The idea began with a concrete problem: the founder spent hours studying RF communications slides and searching videos, websites, and ChatGPT without resolving their confusion. The need is an explanation that fits the course's notation and the student's missing understanding, followed by a way to check whether it helped.

The long-term goal is a companion available throughout the learning cycle: capture permitted lecture material, organize it into understandable notes and concept maps, explain what the student is reading, preserve explanations and visuals with that material, and guide practice using evidence from the student's attempts. Students control when it listens, sees their screen, connects to an account, or offers help.

Our core value is **continuity with the course and the student's work**. Keeping sources, explanations, annotations, and practice together should reduce repeated setup and help the student make progress. Whether this creates a meaningful advantage over ChatGPT and existing study tools remains a hypothesis to test.

The first product is a Mac menu-bar companion: activate it where you already study, keep a floating tutor alongside your lecture, and bring back the right explanation as you revisit a slide. PDF import and saved work support this integration. A separate study platform is not the intended primary experience. Free, open-source components and local inference are current constraints. Correct feedback, honest uncertainty, and student control take priority over adding more features.

Success means students can explain or apply a concept independently and choose to return for another study session. A polished interface, generated notes, or investor interest alone would not establish that success.
